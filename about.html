About Us

A small publication about ideas worth thinking slowly about.

Who we are

Plaintext is an independent blog founded in 2024 by a small group of writers, engineers, and researchers who grew tired of hot takes and wanted somewhere to put longer thoughts. We publish one essay a week — no more, no less.

We believe that the internet has enough fast content. Our goal is to be the opposite: considered, unhurried, and worth re-reading.

What we cover

Technology and its effects on work and society. Productivity, focus, and the psychology of deep work. Occasionally: books, cities, and the strange logic of how people spend their time.
